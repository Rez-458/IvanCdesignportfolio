FAQ

You ask us this a lot.

Will polishing damage my paint?

No. We measure paint thickness in over a dozen spots first and only remove what is needed. Where the paint is thin, we do not polish at all.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Up to 5 years if you hand wash or use touchless washes. Once a year we check the coating for free.

Can I drop the car off and come back in the evening?

Yes. Most packages are done in a day. You can also drop the keys in the morning and collect the next day.

Do I need to wash the car first?

No. Bring it as it is. Washing is always the first step of every package.
